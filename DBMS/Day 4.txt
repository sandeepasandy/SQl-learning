DROP VS DELETE VS TRUNCATE
DELETE: DELETE BASED ON SOME CONDITION DELETE ALL THE RECORDS
DROP : IT DROPS THE ENTIRE TABLE AND STRUCTURE
TRUNCATE : TO DELETE ALL RECORDS PRESENT IN THE TABLE
